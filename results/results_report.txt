   entropy term's share of actor gradient, first -> last : 0.124 -> 0.021
   (entropy_frac = ||beta*dH|| / (||beta*dH|| + ||advantage*dlogp||) per step, averaged
   over the episode -- close to 1.0 means the entropy bonus is dominating the update and
   the return/ES signal is being drowned out; close to 0.0 means entropy has negligible
   influence and the policy is being driven almost entirely by the return/ES objective.)
   NOTE: entropy had not yet reached its target by the final episode.
   Entropy is ABOVE target and beta is pinned at beta_min: the entropy-control
   loop has done everything it can (weakened the entropy bonus as far as
   allowed) and entropy is *still* rising -- this means the return/ES advantage
   term itself is what's pushing the policy toward higher entropy (more diffuse
   weights), not the entropy bonus. Check entropy_frac (section above): if it's
   small (as here) while entropy keeps drifting away from target, lowering
   beta_min further will not help -- the policy is being driven there by the
   return/ES signal itself.
